⚡ The Solution — EmotionDex

How It Works

STEP 1 — DETECT
Customer sends a message → local NLP model analyzes tone, keywords, and sentiment patterns in real-time.
STEP 2 — CLASSIFY
Detected emotion is mapped to one of 6 Pokémon types (Fire=Anger, Water=Sad, Electric=Joy, etc.) with confidence score.
STEP 3 — PRIORITIZE
Priority score 0-100 assigned. Critical Fire-types trigger Team Rocket alerts. Psychic-types get fast-track queuing.
STEP 4 — RESPOND
AI generates empathetic, mode-appropriate response (Calm / Premium / Crisis) — agent reviews and sends in one click.
STEP 5 — IMPROVE
All interactions feed into actionable learnings — emotional triggers, escalation patterns, and coaching opportunities.
